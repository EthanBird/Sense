VB-CABLE redistribution notice
==============================

Sense Mic for Windows bundles the base VB-CABLE package from VB-Audio Software.
VB-CABLE is donationware by Vincent Burel / VB-Audio Software. All
participations are welcome.

Product and donation page: https://vb-audio.com/Cable/
Origin: https://www.vb-cable.com/
Licensing and distribution terms: https://vb-audio.com/Services/licensing.htm

The official VB-CABLE package is redistributed without modification in the
driver\vb-cable directory. Its original readme.txt contains the end-user license
and copyright notice. VB-CABLE remains a separately identifiable shared Windows
audio component and is not removed automatically when Sense Mic is uninstalled,
because another application may be using it.
